## How each item will be published

### Contract
The full contract address will appear at the top of the site and in the README together, with an explorer link. Anything presented elsewhere before that is unverified.

### Supply
Total and circulating supply, and how each is calculated, with a link to the on-chain read that proves it.

### Fees
Any fee taken on activity or trading, where it is collected, and where it goes. Each split will be shown as a plain table that sums to the whole.

### Allocation
How supply is divided between holders, the workforce, the team and operations. Every allocation will state its holder address, any lock or vesting terms, and the on-chain evidence.

### Staking
If staking is offered: what is staked, what is earned, how rewards are calculated, whether any loyalty or lock effects apply, and how to exit. The staking page on the site stays disabled until these are stated.

## Principles

- **Verifiable.** Each claim links to an address or transaction anyone can check.
- **Complete.** Splits add up. Terms include the awkward parts, such as locks and team share.
- **Unchanging once stated.** If a rule changes after publication, the change is recorded here with the reason.
- **No returns promised.** Value distribution depends on real activity and is not guaranteed.
